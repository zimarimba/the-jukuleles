(06) FAQ

Good to know.

What kind of music do you play?

Soul, funk, pop, Motown and R&B — the classics everyone knows, plus current hits. We read the room, from relaxed dinner sets to a packed dance floor.

How big is the band?

Our core is a four-piece — vocals and guitar, trumpet and keys, bass and drums. We scale up to five, six or seven pieces for bigger rooms and bigger budgets.

Do you play weddings in Singapore?

Yes — weddings are what we’re best known for. We play solemnisations, cocktail hours, wedding banquets and after-parties at hotels and venues across Singapore.

Do you perform at corporate events and dinner & dances?

Yes. We’ve played gala dinners, D&Ds, launches and brand activations for clients including Allianz, Volvo, Mitsubishi Motors, Blancpain, Prudential and The Ritz-Carlton.

Can we request songs?

Absolutely. Send us your must-plays — your first dance, walk-in song or company anthem — and any do-not-plays, and we’ll build the set around them.

How do we book The Jukuleles?

Send us your date, venue and event details using the form below or email hello@thejukuleles.com. We’ll reply with line-up options and a tailored quote. Weekend dates fill up early, so enquire as soon as you have a date.
